# vidformer Code of Conduct

This project follows the Imageomics-style principles of engagement for collaborative research software.

## Our standards

As contributors and maintainers, we commit to:

- **Listen to understand.** Assume good intent and engage with curiosity.
- **Speak to be understood.** Communicate clearly, especially across disciplines and experience levels.
- **Take space / make space.** Invite participation and avoid dominating discussions.
- **Beware of blind spots.** Be open to perspectives, backgrounds, and workflows different from your own.
- **Respect time.** Keep discussions focused and constructive.
- **Care for each other.** Help build a welcoming, collaborative, and humane community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository discussions and contributions, including issues, pull requests, review comments, documentation, and other project spaces.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- insulting, demeaning, or exclusionary language;
- doxxing or sharing private information without permission;
- sustained disruptive behavior in discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainer at [dominikwinecki@gmail.com](mailto:dominikwinecki@gmail.com).

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.
